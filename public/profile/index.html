WHAT IS
GFRP REBAR?

Glass Fibre Reinforced Polymer rebar is an advanced structural composite engineered to permanently replace steel TMT reinforcement in concrete subjected to corrosive environments.

ECR Boron-Free Glass Rovings
High-purity continuous glass rovings aligned longitudinally in the direct axis of tension. Minimum fiber mass content ≥ 75% per IS 18256:2023.
Structural Epoxy / Vinyl Ester Matrix
Crosslinked polymeric matrix encasing every glass filament. 100% free of polyester resin, providing total barrier resistance against alkaline pore solutions (pH 12.5–13.5).
Continuous Helical Spiral Ribbing
Precision mechanical helical winding delivering concrete bond strength ≥ 8 MPa, ensuring crack-width mitigation and full developmental anchorage.
Linear-Elastic Behavior & Coiling
Linear-elastic stress-strain profile up to failure with no yield plateau. Lower flexural stiffness in smaller diameters (4–10 mm) allows elastic coiling with 100% straight elastic recovery on uncoiling.
PROVEN INTERNATIONAL RECORD

GFRP reinforcement has delivered over 50 years of verified performance in North America, Europe, and Japan. Standardized in India under IS 18256:2023 and road guidelines IRC:137-2022.

ALKALI RESISTANCE

Alkaline Pore-Water Durability & Resin Chemistry

Concrete pore solutions maintain sustained high alkalinity (pH 12.5 to 13.5). Rebars formulated with commercial polyester resins undergo severe saponification and tensile degradation over time. RebarX structural reinforcement is engineered exclusively with corrosion-resistant ECR boron-free glass rovings bound in an advanced epoxy resin matrix—100% free of polyester blends. This system easily satisfies the IS 18256:2023 requirement of retaining ≥ 80% tensile capacity following 90 days of accelerated alkaline immersion testing.

CIVIL & PRECAST

BUILDING & CIVIL APPLICATIONS

Where RebarX GFRP replaces steel TMT for permanent corrosion immunity:

Boundary Wall Columns & Plinth Beams

GFRP cages in boundary wall columns, foundation ties, and fence plinths. Zero steel means zero rust spalling, even in aggressive coastal saline fog and damp border zones.

Underground Water Tanks & Basements

100% corrosion-free reinforcement for subterranean RCC tanks, sumps, and retaining basements. Permanently prevents rebar oxidation—the #1 cause of tank leakage and concrete failure.

Plinth Slabs & Grade Beams

Ground-level foundation concrete in high-salinity soil and high water tables, eliminating protective bituminous paint or chemical sacrificial coatings.

Precast Hume Pipes & Non-Magnetic Zones

Collaborating with IIT scholars on composite spirals for NP2–NP4 precast pipes (IS 458 amendment). Completely dielectric for hospital MRI suites and electrical switchyards.

Note: GFRP rebar is not permitted as primary longitudinal reinforcement in main structural multistory building columns under current Indian codes.
